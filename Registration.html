REGISTRATION CLOSES: 5th May 2027

UK DRI member: UK DRI members may register for free. Registrations will be checked against our membership lists.

All other Academic: Registrations are open to all other academic researchers at the same rate (£300).

Non-academic - No Poster: Other attendees (e.g. industry, additional exhibitor representatives) are invited to register at our non-academic rate (£450).

Non-academic + Poster: Non-academic attendees who wish to present a poster at the meeting must register with our non-academic + poster rate (£550).

All registrants can select to participate in an optional conference dinner on 19th May 2027 for an additional £48
